### 6. Overall Quality

Calculate from weighted scores. Scale: 1.0 (very poor) to 6.0 (excellent).
- Instruction Following: 0.25
- Truthfulness: 0.25
- Prompt Correctness: 0.20
- Writing Style: 0.15
- Verbosity: 0.15

weighted_score = IF*0.25 + Truth*0.25 + Correctness*0.20 + Writing*0.15 + Verbosity*0.15
